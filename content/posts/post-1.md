## A garden needs a path

I used to wait for an idea to feel complete before writing it down. Most of them disappeared. Now I keep a small system: capture quickly, connect generously, and return when the thought has had time to grow.

> The goal is not to publish more. It is to notice more.

### The three-part rhythm

- **Collect** fragments without judging them
- **Tend** the notes that keep calling me back
- **Share** only when the idea can help someone else

This garden is less an archive and more a conversation with my past self.
